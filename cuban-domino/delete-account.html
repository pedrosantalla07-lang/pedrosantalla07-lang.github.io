Delete your Cuban Domino account

Cuban Domino · by Pinar Apps

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Cuban Domino and log in to the account.
  2. Tap the settings button (the gear, top right).
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm with Delete permanently.

The account is deleted immediately.

Without the app

Email cubandomino.app@gmail.com from the email address on the account, with the subject "Delete my Cuban Domino account" and your username. We delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account: username, email address and password.
  • Your rank, rating, level, match history, coins and store items.
  • Your friends list, friend requests, blocks and undelivered messages.

What may be kept

  • Reports about other players that you sent, and reports about you, for up to 12 months, to keep the game safe.
  • Server connection logs, for up to 30 days.
  • Public lobby chat is not saved: the server only holds the last few lines in memory, and copies them into a report if someone reports a player.

Progress you made while playing offline as a guest is stored only on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes it.

See also our privacy policy.
