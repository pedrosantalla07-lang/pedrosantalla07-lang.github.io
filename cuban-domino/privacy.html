Privacy Policy

Cuban Domino · Last updated: November 2026

This policy explains what information the Cuban Domino app collects, why, and the choices you have. Cuban Domino is made by Pinar Apps ("we"). Questions: cubandomino.app@gmail.com.

Playing without an account

You can play offline and as a guest without giving us any personal information. Your guest name, settings and offline progress are saved only on your device.

Information we collect

If you create an account

  • Username and email address, to identify your account and let you log in.
  • Password, stored only as a salted, one-way hash. We cannot read it.
  • Game data: your level, rank, rating, match results, coins, store items, chosen avatar, campaign progress you report, friends, friend requests and the players you block.

When you play online

  • Messages you send in lobby chat and direct messages to friends, so they can be delivered. Public chat is not saved; the server holds only the last few lines in memory. Messages to a friend who is offline are kept until they are delivered.
  • Reports: if you report a player, we keep the report and that player's recent lobby chat so we can review it.
  • Connection data such as your IP address, used to keep the connection working and to protect the service from abuse. It is not used for advertising by us.

Purchases

If you buy Remove ads, Google Play handles the payment. We never see your card or payment details; we receive only a purchase confirmation from Google, which we keep on your account so the purchase can be restored.

Password reset emails

If you ask to reset your password, we send a one-time code to your account's email address through our email provider, Brevo. The code expires after 15 minutes.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), device information and how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK) the app asks for your consent first, and you can change that choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

How we use information

  • To run online matches, rankings, friends, chat and the in-game store.
  • To keep play fair and safe: enforcing leaving penalties, rate limits and blocks, reviewing reports, and muting or suspending accounts that break the rules.
  • To show ads that keep the game free.

We do not sell your personal information. Coins and store items in Cuban Domino have no real-money value and cannot be bought or cashed out.

Sharing

Other players can see your username, avatar, level, rank and rating, and what you write in public chat. We share data with service providers only as needed to run the game: Tailscale (United States), which carries the secure connection to our own game server in the United States; Brevo, which delivers password reset emails; Google Play, which handles purchases; and Google AdMob for ads. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Account data: until you delete your account.
  • Undelivered direct messages: until delivered or the account is deleted.
  • Reports: up to 12 months after they are reviewed.
  • Server connection logs: up to 30 days.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings → Delete account), or follow the steps on our account deletion page. Deleting removes your account, rank, coins, store items, friends and saved progress from our servers.

Children

Cuban Domino is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, or to object to its use. Contact us at the address above and we will respond within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
